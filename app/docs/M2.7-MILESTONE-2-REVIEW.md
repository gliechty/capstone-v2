## First-divergence diagnostics

Observations are ordered by dependency stage: selection → benchmark → normalization → base weight → multiplier → question points → category weight → category totals → category score → overall. A failure prints case, question/category location, stage, field, expected/actual, comparison rule, config/method identity and evidence source.

Ten test-only mutations each change one earlier stage and both downstream overall scores. Assertions prove that the earlier cause is reported rather than the overall symptom, with the correct identity/location. Example: altering a numeric question's normalized score by 1 reports normalization for that question, even when raw and weighted overall are also wrong. Synthetic mutations never modify the engine, persisted config or fixtures.
