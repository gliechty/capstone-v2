## Validation contract and its limits

The original user-facing JSON shape remains executive_summary, up to five ranked priority_recommendations, up to three key_gaps and grounding_notes. Strict schemas reject extra keys and invalid types/categories. Ranks must be contiguous; duplicate titles, oversized actions, internal IDs and narrative URLs are rejected. Citation tuples must exactly match a supplied chunk's canonical document title, issuer, recorded page/section and safe HTTPS link. URL whitespace is trimmed only for presentation, never in canonical storage. Invented titles, pages, issuers, links and citations to withheld sources fail.

The executive summary must end with a supplied exact sentence containing the raw and weighted overall scores at the accepted one-decimal display precision. It is validated, not inserted or repaired after generation. Full-precision numeric data remains in the prompt/result; presentation values never feed calculations. Other numeric score claims are rejected by conservative lexical checks. Percentage statements elsewhere must be present in raw percentage answers or supplied excerpts. Relevance checks require explicit industry, employee band and business goal in the summary, answered categories only, and coverage of at least one lowest observed category among recommendations. Evidence-free recommendations must use the original bounded-inference wording; grounding notes must acknowledge unverified evidence.

These checks establish structure, references and mechanical consistency. They do **not** prove factual entailment, legal applicability, quality of advice, appropriateness of every proposed action or exhaustive semantic detection of all possible prose contradictions. Lexical checks can reject legitimate paraphrases; the strict closing/context format needs live-model evaluation. No real provider has been tested and no recommendation quality, latency or public-deployment approval is claimed. M3.4 must preserve provenance/unavailable states rather than treating these checks as a factual certification.

## Bounds and failure behavior

Versioned local policy: 400,000 request bytes, 400,000 prompt bytes, 40,000 response bytes, 4,000 maximum output tokens and a 10,000 ms provider deadline. Limits are validated within explicit hard ceilings; the HTTP body has a separate ten-second read deadline. No retries. A provider that ignores AbortSignal still cannot keep the caller waiting beyond the deadline; late settlement cannot replace the returned unavailable result. A future live adapter must also cap streamed response bytes before buffering and honor cancellation.

Explicit unavailable codes cover missing provider, no answers, insufficient evidence, oversized prompt, timeout, cancellation, provider failure and invalid output. Validation diagnostics contain safe codes/paths rather than raw provider content. Request/config errors produce safe HTTP errors, never stack traces or provider messages. Input identity is a SHA-256 of the parsed raw request with stable answer-key ordering; derived results are not persisted or cached. M3.4 will own client-side stale-response integration.
