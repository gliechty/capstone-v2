Follow one requirement into the question bank (about 2 minutes). Illustrative animation built from MOSAIC’s configuration files.

MOSAIC’s question bank is built from requirements and guidance in source documents.

Let’s follow one.

This question comes from the CDP questionnaire for small and medium enterprises:

Does your organization have an environmental policy that addresses environmental issues?

This question, together with its follow-up options and guidance, identifies several distinct things:

whether a policy exists, and whether it covers the company’s operations, suppliers, and downstream activities.

Each one is recorded as an atomic indicator, small enough to answer on its own.

Each keeps a source reference and a supporting excerpt.

Then the same idea is looked for across the whole knowledge base.

Related requirements about policies, targets and tracking are grouped together: twenty-three of them, from eleven of the forty-four source documents.

Grouping means a business isn’t asked the same thing over and over, in different words.

That recurrence sets the base weight.

The weighting combines two signals: how widely the theme appears across documents, and how frequently it appears overall.

Their relative ranks receive seventy-five and twenty-five percent respectively, then are scaled from one to five.

Here, that gives three point eight six out of five.

The group becomes plain questions.

Does your company have a written environmental policy that applies to your own operations? And does it also cover suppliers, and customers?

Each one gets an answer scale, a category, and a rule for who is asked. This one goes to every business, of every size.

All of this is stored as configuration: readable settings the app uses in every assessment.

Change the documents, and the questions and weights could be rebuilt the same way.
